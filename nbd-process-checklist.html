01
Lead Pipeline & Qualification
Personality-fit qualification before any commitment
Owns the entire lead pipeline — inquiries, MLS-monitoring outreach, referrals, sourced leads. Decides whether Verandah is the right fit using DISC and pattern recognition.
Pre-PMA
✓ Complete
  • Lead intake & first contact
    Capture inquiry source (Google, referral, BBB, MLS, etc.) and log in pipeline. Initial reply within 24 hours, tailored to lead's tone.
  • DISC profile signals & personality-fit read
    Identify whether this owner is a strong fit for Verandah's white-glove model. Target S and C profiles; flag D leads for careful management.
  • Pipeline categorization
    Place in Hot (within 60 days), Warm Nurture (monthly touchpoints), Archive, or hidden former-owner/MLS-monitoring with 60- and 120-day outreach triggers.
  • Custom outreach & nurture
    No templates, no generic scripts. Every conversation tailored to this owner's personality and situation. Document decisions and outreach in pipeline notes.

Pre-Management Engagement
Pre-Management Prep Form & pricing strategy
When the lead is emotionally signaling "yes," send the Pre-Management Prep Form, finalize pricing, and prepare the management agreement.
Pre-PMA
✓ Complete
  • Send Pre-Management Prep Form (Jotform)
    Captures foundational property details and tests owner engagement. Signal of real commitment.
  • Complete rental price analysis
    Use Stellar MLS + CoreLogic 360 Property View data. Build CMA if appropriate. Set rental price.

    Final pricing, customization, signatures collected. Confirm bundle level (Gold vs. Platinum) and any exceptions.

04
AppFolio Skeleton Setup
Owner Page + Property Page basics
Build the AppFolio foundation the team fully populates from. Tags for source & communication preference. Lake Nona Property Group assignment. Document attachments.
Phase 01 · Internal
✓ Complete
  • Build AppFolio Owner Page skeleton
    Owner name, phone, email, physical address, temporary tax ID placeholder (999-99-9999 until W-9 received).
  • Add AppFolio Tags: Source & Communication Preference
    Source tag: how they heard about Verandah (Google, Referral, BBB, MLS, etc.). Communication preference tag: per the management agreement (Email, Text, Phone). Both filterable in reports.
  • Build AppFolio Property Page skeleton
    Property title, type, address, county, year built, bed/bath, square footage, reserve amount, management/leasing/renewal fees, late fee %, $500 maintenance limit, bank account assignments.
  • Attach documents to Property Page
    Signed PMA + Pre-Management Prep Form + New Owner Onboarding Form. All searchable in the Property Page record going forward.
  • Add to Lake Nona Property Group (if applicable)
    For Lake Nona properties only — enables territory-specific reporting and oversight.
  • Forward signed PMA to Athenée & Megan
    Athenée files; Megan reviews for operational flags.
  • Confirm SureVestor PP Bundle status with Athenée
    Default: added to bundle once leased. Exception: Gold-only PMAs (e.g., Ewald) — Pamela proactively notifies Athenée NOT to add.
